Mole Smash assets
=================

Copyright (C) 2026 JavocSoft

The mole character artwork was created specifically for LinuxStreamDeck from
an original prompt. It does not copy or derive from Whac-A-Mole, Mattel, Bob's
Space Racers, or another branded game asset.

The WAV effects used by Mole Smash are original synthesized sounds generated
by tools/generate_game_sounds.py. Running that script reproduces every bundled
audio file without downloading any third-party material.
